## Marketing intel brief

Date researched: 2026-09-15. Domain: organic book discovery and review-page quality.

Google's current high-quality-review guidance recommends original analysis, evidence, and benefits/drawbacks: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/write-high-quality-reviews (page updated 2025-12-10; verified September 15). Confidence: high for published guidance, not a ranking guarantee. Its spam policies prohibit keyword stuffing and scaled content created primarily to manipulate rankings: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies (current page verified September 15).

Application: use accurate book title, author, series number, and relevant genre language naturally in metadata and prose; distinct book-specific critical angles; no unsupported superlatives, keyword quotas, or guaranteed SEO results. Keep source scope and publisher ownership clear. Crossovers are author-supplied canon information, not a claim that every individual volume contains a crossover.

Limitations: no search-volume measurements or Search Console data were supplied; public feedback is a small, nonrepresentative sample. Existing older reviews were not used as independent external evidence. Unrelated legacy articles and current working-tree changes remain outside this campaign.
