## How should you use a LitRPG Con 2027 reader guide to plan authors and signings?

Use a LitRPG Con 2027 reader guide for authors and signings by locking in your top signing window and top panel each morning before adding casual side sessions. Leaving thirty minutes between a basement panel and an upstairs dealer hall queue keeps a ten-minute elevator wait from costing you an autograph.

First-time convention goers often make the mistake of treating the schedule grid like a college course catalog—packing every hour from 10:00 am to 6:00 pm with back-to-back panels. By Saturday at 2:00 pm, they are starving, carrying twelve pounds of books, and stuck at the back of a signing line that started twenty minutes ago.

## How do you choose LitRPG panels that introduce you to your next favorite series?

Choose panels by the story craft question on the card—such as balancing party roles, designing crunchy skill trees, or writing high-stakes dungeon crawls—rather than only picking rooms where you have already read every panelist. A sharp topic brings out the best banter and shows you how an unfamiliar author thinks.

We all love cheering for the authors whose ten-book series kept us awake until 3:00 am. Go see them! But the magic of a genre-specific convention happens when five authors sit down to argue about system design, monster evolution, or why crafting protagonists are so addictive to read.

When you sit in a topic-driven panel, keep the author profiles open in the app. Because ConRoster's author pages (as of October 18, 2026) display books, covers, and series data straight from [LitRPG Tools](https://litrpgtools.com), you can tap a panelist who just made a hilarious point about loot tables and see their exact Book 1 cover and series order while they are still speaking. If you step up to the audience mic during Q&A, keep your question short and focused on the panel topic so all four or five authors on stage get a chance to answer.

## How do you go from a panel room straight to an author's signing table?

Go from a panel to a signing table by tapping the author's unified profile in the convention app to see their linked dealer hall booth pin, walking directions, and scheduled signing hours. Checking the booth location before the panel ends lets you head straight to the right aisle instead of crowding the stage table.

When a panel finishes at 1:50 pm, volunteers usually need five to ten minutes to clear the stage and set microphones for the 2:00 pm session. Trying to corner an author with three hardcovers at the water pitcher slows down the room turnover and stresses out the panelists.

## What should you pack in your signing bag before heading to the Grand Hyatt?

Pack the one or two books that matter most to you in protective sleeves, write your preferred personalization spelling on a sticky note stuck inside the front cover, and check the convention's published item limits before joining the queue. Traveling light keeps your shoulders fresh for three days of panels and quests.

Authors love signing books and hearing which scene or character hooked you, but a forty-person signing line moves best when readers come prepared:
* **Verify the published signing rules first:** Always check the official LitRPG Con program for when an author is signing and whether there is a per-person book limit for peak queues.
* **Use the sticky-note trick:** Put a small note on the title page with your name spelled clearly in block letters. The author never has to guess over noisy dealer hall chatter, and you get thirty extra seconds to talk about the story.
* **Ask before snapping photos:** A quick "May I grab a photo across the table?" gives the author and their booth volunteer a clear, polite cue.
